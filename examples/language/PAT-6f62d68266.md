# Rehearse the stop rule

PAT-6f62d68266 · revision 2 · CC0-1.0

## Context

A bounded release with observable service signals.

Evidence: [EXC-9b2dbed4a3 r1](#evidence-exc-9b2dbed4a3-1)

## Problem

A team recognizes failure too late to reverse a change.

Evidence: [EXC-9b2dbed4a3 r1](#evidence-exc-9b2dbed4a3-1)

## Forces

Release speed; reversibility; confidence in monitoring.

Evidence: [EXC-9b2dbed4a3 r1](#evidence-exc-9b2dbed4a3-1)

## Solution

Before release, specify a measurable stop signal, its observer, a rollback owner, and a decision time; rehearse the response.

Evidence: [EXC-9b2dbed4a3 r1](#evidence-exc-9b2dbed4a3-1)

## Resulting Context

A release has an executable route back to a known state.

Evidence: [EXC-9b2dbed4a3 r1](#evidence-exc-9b2dbed4a3-1)

## Benefits

The team can act on a signal without renegotiating roles.

Evidence: [EXC-9b2dbed4a3 r1](#evidence-exc-9b2dbed4a3-1)

## Costs

Rehearsal effort and monitoring overhead.

Evidence: [EXC-9b2dbed4a3 r1](#evidence-exc-9b2dbed4a3-1)

## Instances

Fictional urgent patch and second rehearsal.

Evidence: [EXC-9b2dbed4a3 r1](#evidence-exc-9b2dbed4a3-1)

## Exceptions

Irreversible migrations require a different recovery strategy.

Evidence: [EXC-9b2dbed4a3 r1](#evidence-exc-9b2dbed4a3-1)

## Boundaries

Assumption: signals are timely and rollback is valid. External technical review is needed for thresholds. The interview does not establish causal effectiveness.

Evidence: [EXC-9b2dbed4a3 r1](#evidence-exc-9b2dbed4a3-1)

## Controls

Candidate technical control: change recovery testing. Possible evidence: stop criteria and dated rehearsal record.

Evidence: [EXC-9b2dbed4a3 r1](#evidence-exc-9b2dbed4a3-1)

### EXC-9b2dbed4a3 r1

Fictional incident: before deploying, the team wrote down a queue-delay threshold, a rollback owner, and a decision time. Speed competed with reversibility. A rehearsal made the threshold usable. The patch completed; one earlier test failed because nobody watched the signal. Monitoring and rehearsal cost staff time. This practice recurs in bounded releases, but fails if rollback is impossible or monitoring is stale. A second rehearsal used the same form. Candidate evidence: approved stop criteria and rehearsal record; technical validation is required.
