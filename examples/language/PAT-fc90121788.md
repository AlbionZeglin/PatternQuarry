# An acknowledged handoff

PAT-fc90121788 · revision 2 · CC0-1.0

## Context

Work passes between shifts or operating teams.

Evidence: [EXC-7960b1aa42 r1](#evidence-exc-7960b1aa42-1)

## Problem

A sender mistakes a sent message for shared understanding.

Evidence: [EXC-7960b1aa42 r1](#evidence-exc-7960b1aa42-1)

## Forces

Completeness; attention; continuity of ownership.

Evidence: [EXC-7960b1aa42 r1](#evidence-exc-7960b1aa42-1)

## Solution

Send a short receipt naming the change, watch signals, and next owner; obtain an explicit acknowledgement.

Evidence: [EXC-7960b1aa42 r1](#evidence-exc-7960b1aa42-1)

## Resulting Context

The receiving team has accepted the next action.

Evidence: [EXC-7960b1aa42 r1](#evidence-exc-7960b1aa42-1)

## Benefits

Ambiguity is visible before the sender leaves.

Evidence: [EXC-7960b1aa42 r1](#evidence-exc-7960b1aa42-1)

## Costs

A summary must be maintained and acknowledged.

Evidence: [EXC-7960b1aa42 r1](#evidence-exc-7960b1aa42-1)

## Instances

Two fictional rehearsed shift handoffs.

Evidence: [EXC-7960b1aa42 r1](#evidence-exc-7960b1aa42-1)

## Exceptions

An acknowledgement cannot confer missing authority.

Evidence: [EXC-7960b1aa42 r1](#evidence-exc-7960b1aa42-1)

## Boundaries

Assumption: the receiver can act. Seek a counterexample from an unstaffed shift. Acknowledgement is not evidence of effectiveness.

Evidence: [EXC-7960b1aa42 r1](#evidence-exc-7960b1aa42-1)

## Controls

Candidate operational control: continuity of responsibility. Possible evidence: dated handoff receipt.

Evidence: [EXC-7960b1aa42 r1](#evidence-exc-7960b1aa42-1)

### EXC-7960b1aa42 r1

Fictional incident: the handoff included a short receipt listing the change, signals to watch, the next owner, and an acknowledgement. A long email had previously gone unread. Completeness competed with attention. The receipt reduced ambiguity in two rehearsed handoffs, with the cost of maintaining a concise summary. It helps across shifts; it fails when the receiving team lacks authority or cannot acknowledge. Candidate evidence: acknowledged handoff record; do not treat acknowledgement as proof of effectiveness.
