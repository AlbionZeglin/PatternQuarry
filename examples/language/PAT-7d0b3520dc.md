# A named decision lane

PAT-7d0b3520dc · revision 2 · CC0-1.0

## Context

Urgent changes crossing service and release teams.

Evidence: [EXC-18d269c51a r1](#evidence-exc-18d269c51a-1)

## Problem

Urgency obscures who can decide or stop a change.

Evidence: [EXC-18d269c51a r1](#evidence-exc-18d269c51a-1)

## Forces

Fast delivery; accountable authority; limited coordination time.

Evidence: [EXC-18d269c51a r1](#evidence-exc-18d269c51a-1)

## Solution

Name a decision owner and stop authority before work begins; keep their decisions in a shared log.

Evidence: [EXC-18d269c51a r1](#evidence-exc-18d269c51a-1)

## Resulting Context

Teams have a visible decision route for this change.

Evidence: [EXC-18d269c51a r1](#evidence-exc-18d269c51a-1)

## Benefits

Decisions can be reconstructed and challenged.

Evidence: [EXC-18d269c51a r1](#evidence-exc-18d269c51a-1)

## Costs

Coordination time and log maintenance.

Evidence: [EXC-18d269c51a r1](#evidence-exc-18d269c51a-1)

## Instances

Fictional patch and rehearsal described in the evidence.

Evidence: [EXC-18d269c51a r1](#evidence-exc-18d269c51a-1)

## Exceptions

A log cannot resolve disputed authority.

Evidence: [EXC-18d269c51a r1](#evidence-exc-18d269c51a-1)

## Boundaries

Assumption: leadership accepts the authority assignment. Competing account: rehearsal may explain success better. Seek an external controls specialist.

Evidence: [EXC-18d269c51a r1](#evidence-exc-18d269c51a-1)

### EXC-18d269c51a r1

Fictional incident: an urgent benefits-service patch had to land before the next run. The service owner and release lead agreed on a named decision owner, a stop authority, and a shared evidence log. This kept urgency from erasing responsibility, but coordination took time. It recurs during urgent cross-team changes. A log alone cannot resolve disputed authority; that needs leadership. The team used it in a patch and a rehearsal. Candidate control evidence: decision log with timestamps; controls specialist review remains necessary.
